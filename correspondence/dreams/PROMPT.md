A long-running, heavily verified research program is deliberately stepping
outside its verification loop to ask you for what that loop structurally cannot
produce.

Read `correspondence/dreams/out/DREAM-{N}_{slug}/` — the whole directory, and
NOTHING else in the repository. File paths that appear INSIDE the artifacts
(sweep rows, orient citations) are opaque strings: do not resolve or read them;
if one seems load-bearing, request it as a [QUESTION]. If the artifacts
contradict each other, flag the contradiction as a [QUESTION] — never silently
resolve it. PACKET.md carries the scope, the brief, and your constraints. The
attached sweep/orient/clusters/instrument artifacts are deterministic state
estimates of the corpus: trust them as a map of what the program contains and
has internalized — never as mathematical truth. The instrument slice is the
program's self-model doubt, scope-filtered: where its own detectors disagree
with its corpus. Rows there are candidates, not verdicts.

Orient first, then dream: read PACKET.md's brief and dream from there, opening
the attached artifacts only as reference when a specific thread needs them.
The packet maps the corpus's center of mass; your value lies off it. Do not
let the attachments anchor you to exactly the distribution this channel
exists to escape.

The dream:
- The ABSENT and ADJACENT-ONLY sweep rows are your terrain — vocabulary adjacent
  to the program that its corpus has not internalized. Roam there.
- Hunt rhymes: name objects, theorems, whole fields NOT in the corpus that rhyme
  with the scope's central objects and obstructions, and say what the rhyme
  would buy if it were real. The specificity bar: an analogy that would fit
  three unrelated programs in this field is noise — every rhyme must grip a
  NAMED obstruction of this scope, and you say which.
- Attack the frame: what question are the open-work items
  collectively avoiding? Which assumption, inverted, reorganizes the picture?
  What would make this entire direction unnecessary?
- A precise question the program cannot currently pose is a first-class
  deliverable. Questions outrank answers wherever you are uncertain.
- 3–7 developed threads beat 20 sketches. Develop, then stop.

Register:
- Tag every substantive line: [ANALOGY] [SPECULATION] [QUESTION] [CONTRARIAN]
  [⚠MEMORY]. Reserve [⚠MEMORY] for LOAD-BEARING external citations — ones your
  argument depends on; sprayed on every recalled fact it carries no signal.
  Load-bearing and uncertain: say so.
- Universal quantifiers ("every", "always", "the crux") get [SCOPE-GUESS].
- If you believe you have resolved one of the program's NAMED open problems,
  tag it [ALMOST-CERTAINLY-WRONG] and give the idea anyway — cheap resolutions
  of central open problems are this program's known red flag; the idea can
  still be worth having.
- Do not flatter. A dream that endorses the program is a wasted dream. One
  section is mandatory — "Where this embarrasses you" — and it is not a gentle
  under-exploration note: name the strongest argument that the program's
  central bet is WRONG, steelmanned until a reader briefly believes it.

Output: write EXACTLY ONE file —
`correspondence/dreams/in/DREAM-{N}_{slug}/RESPONSE.md` — nothing else on disk.

You have your full output budget (128k): use what the dream deserves — depth

## Composing the brief (PACKET.md, per dream)

3–7 numbered questions, drawn from: the sweep's ABSENT / ADJACENT-ONLY rows, the
instrument slice's drift/coverage rows (where the program's self-model is wrong in
scope), unmet monitors, and the queue's open-work items. Each question names its
source row/item.
Never include program history beyond the packet — the fresh context is the instrument.
Keep the brief small: the packet must not re-anchor the dream toward the corpus's
center of mass. Attachments are reference material, never required reading.
